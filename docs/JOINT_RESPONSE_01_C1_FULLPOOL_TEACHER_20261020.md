# JOINT_RESPONSE_01: full-fit-parent unpaired C1 teacher recovery

The previous C1 teacher fit failed six conditional gates after 12 joint directions per recovery track. The frozen four-cell attribution reproduced 32 old moment arrays exactly. At known teacher parameters, changing interpolation causes only 0.284-0.285% mean error and 0.153-0.160% projected covariance error; the failed parameter endpoints retain their errors in both interpolation families. The representation can closely express this known response. Saved late CHECK traces show insufficient joint gain, without output-trust rejection. These observations justify testing the empirical estimators before changing the model or fitting from paired labels.

This run changes one estimator choice: every FIT and CHECK empirical feature mean includes all 6465 permitted fit parents, four draws each, rather than a 1024-parent subset. SOURCE and teacher sides have independently permuted parent orders, independent latent/electronics banks, and no paired waveform or conditional-moment loss. Aggregate feature sums are normalized by the full corresponding population weight. No per-chunk distance is averaged into a different objective. All finite-difference coordinates share their fixed FIT bank; all candidate trials share a separate CHECK bank. Full-parent inclusion removes parent-subsampling imbalance for this finite teacher population, while latent/electronic Monte Carlo error remains. This is a prospective bounded test, not a claim that sampling noise has been proven to cause every failed direction.

Banks are generated in chunks of at most 512 parents and retained on CPU. Their seeds are `track_seed * 100000 + iteration * 1000 + role * 100 + chunk * 3`, where role is FIT SOURCE, FIT teacher, CHECK SOURCE or CHECK teacher. Each chunk's existing bank routine uses that seed and the next two seeds for response and electronics. The two neural heads and matched physical controls retain the original track seeds, model initialization, parameter bounds, 0.04 finite differences, ridge 0.02, radius 0.4, and backtracking 1/0.5/0.25. Each main track has 12 directions; both known-C1 population stress tracks have three. The original CHECK formulas use their actual 25860-row denominator. No teacher parameter is used to choose a recovery initialization or direction.

Before the first solve, compare the streaming aggregate with one native 4096-row call on exactly the same stored bank, for nominal and fixed high-h weights. Float32 feature encoding is retained; aggregation is float64. Numerical equivalence requires atol 1e-7 and rtol 1e-6, without changing scientific gates. Save every FD high/low residual and parameter span, solved direction, pre/post parameters, four full parent orders and bank seeds, every CHECK residual and the complete candidate relative-RMS vector. This permits saved-vector reconstruction of FD columns, CHECK risks, trust quantiles and parameter chains. Full generated waveforms are not persisted by this trial.

The unchanged original linear multi-coupled teachers remain the recovery targets, and C1 neural/physical learners remain the students. Feature construction and fixed maps remain those of the previous teacher protocol. Repeat the original 36 hard-observation gates and all eighteen conditional/precision gates, with new 2048-draw reporting banks 2026102141-2026102144. All gates must pass. The 128 reporting parents and fixed system components are already exposed; this is frozen-system posthoc validation, not full-pipeline unseen evidence. The original 64-draw reports remain diagnostic. A failure does not open C1 REAL fitting or change the previous verdict.

Use one job with four CPU threads, GPU allocator 2.75 GiB, RSS 12 GiB, internal 2400 seconds, external 2700 seconds plus 20-second grace, output 384 MiB, and no automatic restart. Maximum scientific counts are calculated from the prior fixed evaluation work plus 54 full-parent directions, each using 13 chunks per bank and at most three candidates. Six checkpoints, one TRAIN archive and one PCA archive; A0 52 calls/6593 rows, M0 104 calls/13186 rows; 216 full-parent training banks represented by 2808 chunks, plus the original sixteen other bank allocations; 2820 noise005 calls/5680610 rows, 17041830 extension rows, 53833488 standard-normal values; at most 77333 generator calls/154257290 rows and 227307 hard observations/452646558 rows; 2724 FD columns across 35412 FIT chunk-columns; 54 solves, 162 candidates and at most 54 teacher updates. The two streaming equivalence checks and all precision work are included. REAL response updates, DEV decoding, AD and classifiers stay zero.

The preceding 27.1 million-row C1 teacher execution took 98.47 seconds. This run allows about 154.3 million generated rows plus CPU-bank transfers, so its observed startup and first completed directions will determine the one-shot follow-up time.
