# JOINT_RESPONSE_01 native-constrained joint direction diagnostic

Prospective stage-4 mechanism test following the failed REAL fit, constrained-direction test and fixed-ray linearization diagnostic. The latter reproduces the original residual exactly and shows substantial hard-readout nonlinearity on FIT itself, with additional FIT/CHECK projection differences. Reducing a step can reduce joint risk while preserving native metrics approximately, but has not established useful native-wave improvement.

## Registered change

Keep the same 54 response/electronics parameters, original count population, M0 initialization, full joint feature residual, covariance shrinkage, finite-difference displacement 0.04, ridge 0.02 and radius 0.4. No new random variables, network weights or waveform templates are introduced. The quadratic objective and its cross-module terms remain unchanged. Retain the 12 original local macro inequalities and parameter box/trust ball. Add affine first-order nonincrease constraints for the existing native wave, QPSD and noise losses in all three noise hypotheses. Their derivatives are estimated together with the original full Jacobian, using the same plus/minus hard outputs and common random numbers. Dividing each affine constraint by its current positive distance only sets its numerical scale; it does not reweight the objective.

The native losses use the exact functions and previously exposed native evaluation projections from the original full report. They are now explicitly part of direction fitting. Their later reporting is not independent validation. New C2ST, new random libraries, unused feature projections and suitable heldout roles remain necessary for scientific assessment.

The SLSQP solver uses analytic derivatives, maximum 200 iterations and ftol 1e-9. A solver failure or infeasible result stops the job; no alternate solver is silently substituted. The saved first FIT residual and Jacobian must reproduce with maximum absolute difference at most 1e-12.

## Hard-output selection and untouched confirmation

For each of the two seeds, form one direction from the original first FIT batch (1024 parents, four draws each, 4096 REAL records). Try fractions 1, 1/2, 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32, 1/64, 1/128 in that order. Stop at the first fraction passing both actual FIT and the original independent CHECK batch. Each uses the unchanged joint acceptance: at least 1% aggregate joint gain, the original macro bounds, parameter bounds, output trust and flag rules. Add a local native guard of at most 1% wave/QPSD increase and at most 10% noise increase in every hypothesis. The 1% guard is a fixed local optimization tolerance, not a scientific success threshold or evidence of improvement. Final REAL requirements, including at least 5% native wave/QPSD improvement, remain exactly as registered before the first REAL run.

After selection, evaluate only that selected displacement on an untouched fitting-role confirmation batch, new RNG and a new random feature projection seed 2026101955. Source selection, noise and REAL selection offsets are respectively +11000, +12000 and +13000 from each original training seed. This confirmation never chooses a fraction or modifies the direction. It uses the same hard acceptance and local native guards. If no fraction passes the selection checks, record failure and no selected displacement. No update or endpoint is committed in either outcome.

Report the full native metrics, undefined and censored counts, and original denominators for CHECK and fresh-confirmation baselines and selected outputs. Confirm the compact native constraint values match the full report to 1e-12. Save both joint/native Jacobians, constraints, direction, sampled indices and every attempted hard check. All original 4096 generated and 4096 target rows remain included in each comparison. Research heldout SOURCE128 and REAL42 groups/2099 rows, DEV and AD are not used. A0/M0/005/PCA/native standardization retain their known full-TRAIN exposure. This small diagnostic does not claim full-TRAIN iteration coverage.

## Budget and decision

At most two direction solves, 108 finite-difference columns and 16 fraction trials. Six banks: 24576 noise005 rows, 73728 extension rows and 196608 shape-normal values. At most 256 response calls / 1048576 rows and 768 hard-observation calls / 3145728 rows. Compact native calculations use at most 6291456 comparison rows; full reports at most 196608 comparison rows. Setup is six checkpoints, one TRAIN archive, one PCA archive, 52 A0 calls / 6593 rows, 104 M0-head calls / 13186 rows, one existing REAL replay / 21047 rows and three maps. Target feature rows: 8192 FIT and 16384 CHECK/fresh. Zero committed response updates, classifiers, DEV reads and AD queries.

Four CPU threads, 2.75 GiB GPU allocator, 12 GiB RSS, 600 s internal / 720 s external wall limit and 32 MiB output; no automatic restart. Correct startup permits ending the turn and continuing through the existing ten-minute heartbeat.

Native exit zero, source/output hashes, budgets, unchanged roles, full/compact metric parity and saved-matrix feasibility are execution checks. Both seeds must select a nonzero direction and pass untouched confirmation for this diagnostic to support a separately registered training pilot. That success alone cannot satisfy stage 4 or 5. Failure requires inspecting whether infeasible directions, local curvature, data variation or projection variation caused rejection; do not weaken gates, increase capacity, rerun old S or turn this into a parameter sweep.
