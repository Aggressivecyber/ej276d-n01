# JOINT_RESPONSE_01: frozen C1 teacher-family attribution

The C1 teacher run completed, and the independent saved-evidence audit confirmed 54 solves, 81 proposals, 43 accepted teacher updates and all role/parameter chains. Its 36 hard-observation gates passed, but six of eighteen conditional/precision gates failed. Seed0 underproduced canonical charge variance (0.474-0.477 of the teacher); seed1 retained mean error 0.0525-0.0526 and covariance error 0.503-0.505. No changed-generator REAL fit is authorized by that result.

The saved CHECK trace shows that late neural proposals mostly fail the joint-gain rule, without failing output trust. This is compatible with noisy or inaccurate directions but does not separate that issue from the change of interpolation family. Before changing fitting, cross the unchanged original linear teacher and the C1 generator with two frozen parameter vectors: the known teacher vector and the failed fitted endpoint. This gives four cells for each head. No parameter is optimized, no new candidate is selected, and paired teacher labels are used only in this diagnostic.

Reuse exactly the exposed 128 reporting parents and both registered 2048-draw banks per head. Recompute original/truth and C1/endpoint and require exact equality of all 32 saved mean, covariance, Q-variance and wave-variance arrays. Evaluate the two missing cells, C1/truth and original/endpoint, with the same draws. Save all four cells' moment arrays, and compare their actual generated means, 16-cosine covariances and variance summaries. Decompose each total C1-endpoint minus original-truth discrepancy into family-at-truth and parameter-at-C1 terms, retaining the cross term and the two-factor interaction. This is an order-dependent algebraic attribution, not a proof of unique causal or parameter identifiability.

The evidence question is whether the family difference alone is small compared with the failed endpoint discrepancy. A small family difference supports inspecting the unpaired solver's estimators; a large difference requires reconsidering representational compatibility before fitting. Neither outcome relaxes the failed gates, proves REAL response matching, or authorizes a parameter correction from paired labels.

One job, four CPU threads, 2.75 GiB GPU allocator, 12 GiB RSS, 300-second internal deadline, 420-second external deadline plus 20-second termination grace; 32 MiB output, no restart. Maximum work is six checkpoint loads, one TRAIN archive and one PCA archive, A0 52 calls/6593 rows, M0 104 calls/13186 rows, four latent banks, 8388608 standard-normal values, 2048 generator calls/4194304 rows. This explicitly includes 1024 calls/2097152 rows used to replay the existing cells. No electronics draws, hard observations, finite differences, solves, updates, DEV decoding or classifiers.
